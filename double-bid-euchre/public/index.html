HOUSEY

Private online games · 4 players · double deck

House rules
  • 4 players, partners across. 48-card double deck (two 9–A sets), 12 cards each, 12 tricks.
  • Single-round bidding starts left of the dealer. Minimum bid 6. Outbid or pass.
  • Stick the Dealer: If everyone passes, the dealer is stuck with a minimum 6-bid.
  • Farmer's Hand: Holding 8 or more 9s and 10s allows a player to request a redeal during bidding.
  • High bidder names the trump suit and leads the first trick.
  • HOUSEY: Bid 12 to take all 12 tricks. Made = +24, set = -12.
  • BIG HOUSEY: Bid 12 alone to take all 12 tricks. Made = +48, set = -24.
  • No-Trump ("High", Aces High) may only be named on a HOUSEY bid.
  • Right Bower (J of trump) is highest, then Left Bower (J of same color). Two identical cards: first played wins the trick.
  • Scoring: Make your bid = 1 point per trick taken. Miss it = minus your bid amount. Defenders score 1 point per trick taken.
  • First team to 62 points wins.
